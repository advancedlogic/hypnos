### Findings

- The two `entities` 500s ("model 'gliner2.5-multi' is not loaded") happened at 17:02:10, in the
  3.8 s between the scheduler booking Tier 1 for GLiNER and the engine finishing its load: the
  fast path trusted the booking. Fixed as BUG-2 (the fast path asks the engine `is_resident()`
  first); the 12-minute run in `soak-2026-10-30-after-bug2.md` is the confirmation. The
  two-minute shakedown before this run had found BUG-1 the same way (an eviction unloading a
  model between a fast-path lease and its request).
- "VRAM unaccounted flat" failed on a percentage of a near-zero value (0.5 GB -> 0.0 GB); the
  verdict now has an absolute floor of 1 GB.
- The image generations took 137 s each: with four chat clients the 26B is never idle, so
  MOSS-Image could not evict it and ran with a partial GPU placement. Expected under this mix;
  `priority` and `min_gpu_layer_ratio` are the knobs (see `docs/operating.md`).
- Process footprint settled at 31.1-31.5 GB after the warm-up (+1.3 % over 38 minutes), VRAM
  bookings stayed at 24.5 GB with 0.5-2.6 GB really free, Tier 2 at 28.4 GB; the request-log ring
  held at its 200 entries; 261 documents were stored and 257 released by the client.
